FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my resume uploaded anywhere?

No. All analysis happens locally in your browser with JavaScript. Your resume text is never sent to a server — there isn't even a server to send it to. You can verify this by disconnecting from the internet after the page loads; it still works.

How accurate is the match score?

The score measures how much of the job description's weighted keyword vocabulary appears in your resume, with extra weight on tools, skills, and multi-word phrases. It's a strong signal for ATS-style filtering, not a guarantee — no tool can promise an interview. Use it to catch blind spots before you apply.

Should I just stuff keywords into my resume?

No — add keywords only where they're true. Recruiters spot stuffing instantly, and lying about skills backfires in interviews. The suggestions tell you where each gap matters so you can represent real experience in the posting's language.

Why a one-time payment instead of a subscription?

Job hunting is temporary (hopefully). A $4.99 one-time unlock is cheaper and more honest than a subscription you'll forget to cancel the month you get hired.
